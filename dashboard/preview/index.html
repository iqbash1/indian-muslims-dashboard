Infant Mortality Rate

canonical/imr.csv · sources/nfhs-5/reports/india-report-fr375.pdf (Table 7.2, p. 284) · weighted with sources/census-2011/c-series/c01-population-by-religion.xls

Data current to · NFHS-5 (2019-21); 5-year reference period 2014-2020

33.0

All-India Muslim, NFHS-5. Hindu: 35.4, All: 34.7

Infant deaths per 1000 live births. NFHS-5 Table 7.2 publishes URBAN and RURAL IMR by religion separately (no total-residence-by-religion column). Total residence computed as population-weighted average using Census 2011 urban/rural population by religion (urban/rural population shares by religion are stable 2011-2021, <1% drift). Note: Muslim IMR running below Hindu IMR is the well-documented "Muslim mortality paradox" in Indian demography — Muslim infant survival outperforms Muslim adult socioeconomic indicators.

Institutional delivery rate

canonical/inst-delivery.csv · sources/nfhs-5/reports/india-report-fr375.pdf (Table 8.13, p. 324)

Data current to · NFHS-5 (2019-21)

84.30%

All-India Muslim, NFHS-5. Hindu: 89.50%, All: 88.58%

Percentage of live births (5 years preceding NFHS-5) that took place in a health facility. Muslim institutional delivery (84.3%) runs 5.2pp below Hindu (89.5%) — unlike IMR and anaemia (where the Muslim outcome runs ahead), this is the expected direction. Suggests the maternal-care system reaches Muslim women less effectively even where infant outcomes are paradoxically better.

Any anaemia (haemoglobin <12.0 g/dl non-pregnant, <11.0 g/dl pregnant), altitude-adjusted. Total residence, national. Muslim MPs in the 18th Lok Sabha (2024). 4.42% of 543 seats vs Muslim population share ~14.2% — a chronic 9-10pp underrepresentation.

Time series 2009–2024: 28 (5.16%), 22 (4.05%), 27 (4.97%), 24 (4.42%). Muslim prison population — count and incarceration rate

canonical/prison-share.csv · sources/ncrb-prison/psi-2022.pdf (Tables 2.10C–2.13C, pp. 103, 107, 111, 115)

Data current to · NCRB Prison Statistics India 2022 (as on 2023-12-01)

108,968

All-India Muslim prison count. 63.26 per 100,000 Muslims, vs Hindu 39.79 per 100k and All 44.61 per 100k. Muslim rate is 1.59× the Hindu rate.

Combined convicts + undertrials + detenues + other prisoners. Presented as absolute count and as incarceration rate per 100,000 of religious population (a more direct measure of disproportion than "share of prisoners" alone). Muslim incarceration rate of 63.3 per 100k vs Hindu 39.8 per 100k means a Muslim Indian is 1.59× as likely to be in prison as a Hindu Indian. Detenues (preventive-detention prisoners) skew much higher: 40.5% Muslim by share, driven heavily by J&K. Caveat: Maharashtra did not report religion breakdown for ~33k undertrials/detenues — rates are computed over religion-reported prisoners with full population in the denominator (so the actual rate is mildly understated).

83,968

All-India Muslim undertrial count. 48.75 per 100,000 Muslims, vs Hindu 29.33 per 100k and All 33.15 per 100k. Muslim rate is 1.66× the Hindu rate.

Undertrials are prisoners awaiting trial — not convicted. Muslim undertrial rate (48.7 per 100k Muslims) is 1.66× the Hindu rate (29.3 per 100k Hindus), the highest disproportion among the prison categories — consistent with widely-documented patterns of detention-vs-conviction disparity (Muslims face higher pre-trial detention even when conviction rates are similar).

Communal/religious rioting incidents (NCRB)

canonical/communal-incidents-govt.csv · sources/ncrb-crime/cii-2022-book1.pdf (Tables 1.2 + 1A.4)

Data current to · NCRB Crime in India 2022

272

Communal/religious rioting incidents recorded by NCRB nationally in 2022. Time series: 857 (2020) → 378 (2021) → 272 (2022). The published decline is contested by civic compilations which report higher counts; several states have stopped recording 'communal' as a separate category since ~2017.

National annual total of cases registered under "Communal/Religious" rioting (IPC Sec.147-151 sub-classification). The 2020 spike (857) coincides with CAA-NRC protests and the Delhi riots; the subsequent decline (378 → 272) is contested. Several states have stopped recording "communal" as a separate crime category since ~2017, which deflates the national total. Civil-society compilations (Documentation of the Oppressed, India Hate Lab) typically report substantially higher counts. NCRB tables do not disclose religion of victim or perpetrator — only incident counts.
