About this measurement

Definition. Gross Enrolment Ratio = student enrolment in higher education / population in the 18-23 age cohort * 100. The AISHE-published headline GER. Higher education means any course after Class 12: degree, diploma, and certificate programmes at universities, colleges, and standalone institutions.

Methodology. Numerator: AISHE All-India "Muslim Minority" enrolment for two rounds, academic years 2020-21 (Muslim 19,21,713; total 4,13,80,713 per Report p30) and 2021-22 (Muslim 21,08,033; total 4,32,68,181 per Report p29); the 2020-21 All-India cells were recovered by character-level de-interleaving of the Table 15 text layer and match the exact sum of its 36 state rows. Denominator: Census 2011 C-15 (15-19 + 20-24) × 0.6 as an 18-23 effective- cohort proxy, held fixed across both rounds; single-year-age table (C-13) not pulled and 2021 Census deferred. The older 2011 denominator makes our computed GER run a few points ABOVE the officially published figures (national 27-28%; published Muslim Minority GER around 9% in recent AISHE rounds).

The absolute level is a computation, not an official figure, but the cross-religion gap (Muslim enrolment runs at roughly one-third the national rate) is robust to the choice of denominator and is the dashboard story; with the denominator fixed, the small rise between the two rounds is enrolment growth alone. The card also sets Muslims beside "Other minorities" (Christian, Sikh, Buddhist and Jain, 9,05,159 students), which AISHE reports only as one grouped category and never splits: Muslims enrol at roughly three-quarters their rate. AISHE publishes no separate Hindu figure, so there is no Hindu bar.

The by-sex view shows Muslim women enrol at a marginally higher rate than Muslim men; the by-state view ranks Muslim GER across states from enrolment as reported by each state to AISHE (incomplete in some states, so the spread matters more than the exact level).

Sources. AISHE 2021-22 · AISHE 2021-22. Download CSV: ger-higher-ed.csv.
