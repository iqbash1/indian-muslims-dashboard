About this measurement

Definition. Share of population age 15+ who are in the labour force (either working or actively seeking work). Usual status (principal + subsidiary).

Methodology. PLFS 2023-24 Table 48 (pages 396-400). Total residence, person-level. Reference period: Jul 2023 - Jun 2024 (year=2023 in canonical row represents the start of this period).

Sources. PLFS 2023-24. Data file: lfpr-15plus.csv.
