About this measurement

Definition. Share of population aged 7 and above who can both read and write with understanding in any language.

Methodology. Census defines literacy as the ability to read and write with understanding in any language. Two-census series (2001 and 2011, Census C-09 education-by-religion tables); both rounds share the same Census definition, so the 2001 -> 2011 change is a like-for-like comparison. PLFS reports a broadly similar literacy measure on a slightly different definition and is a possible future cross-check; no PLFS series is plotted here today.

Sources. Census 2011 · C-series · Census 2001 · C-series. Data file: lit-7plus.csv.
