About this measurement

Definition. Average monthly spending per person (Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure, MPCE). In India this is the standard measure of economic well-being, used in place of income, for which no reliable figure by religion exists.

Methodology. Sachar Committee (2006), Chapter 8, from the NSS 61st round (2004-05): all-India average MPCE by socio-religious category. Muslims Rs.635 vs the all-India average Rs.712, near SC/ST (Rs.520) and far below upper-caste 'Hindu General' households (Rs.1023). The Urban vs rural and By state tabs come from Sachar Appendix Tables 8.2 (urban) and 8.3 (rural): the Muslim shortfall is wide in towns (Rs.804 vs the Rs.1,105 average) but near parity in villages (Rs.553 vs Rs.579), and as no combined per-state figure is published the state tab shows urban and rural as reported. This is the most recent figure published by religion: later rounds (HCES 2022-23) break spending down by social group (SC/ST/OBC/Others) only, not by religion, so a current religion-wise figure needs the unit-level microdata. Rupees are 2004-05 nominal (not inflation-adjusted).

Sources. Sachar Committee (NSS 2004-05). Data file: mpce.csv.
