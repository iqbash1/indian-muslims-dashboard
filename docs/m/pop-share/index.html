About this measurement

Definition. Muslim population as a share of total population at the given geography level.

Methodology. Population shares from the primary RGI Census religion volumes, 1961 to 2011. Two all-India figures exclude a state where the Census could not be held: 1981 excludes Assam and 1991 excludes Jammu & Kashmir. The 2021 Census is delayed.

Sources. Census 2011 · C-series · Census 1961 · C-VII Religion · Census 1971 · Paper 2 of 1972 · Census 1981 · HH-15 (Paper 3 of 1984) · Census 1991 · C-9 Religion · Census 2001 · C-series. Data file: pop-share.csv.
