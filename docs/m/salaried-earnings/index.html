About this measurement

Definition. Average monthly earnings (gross, preceding calendar month) of workers in regular salaried/wage jobs, age 15+. The pay counterpart to the salaried-jobs card: Muslims are both less likely to hold a regular salaried job and, when they do, are paid less in it.

Methodology. All seven points (2017-18 to 2023-24) are computed from each round's PLFS unit-level microdata via the MoSPI NADA API: the published annual reports never break earnings down by religion. Earnings are those received or receivable during the preceding calendar month from the regular salaried/wage activity (schedule block 6 item 9, asked of persons with current weekly status 31/71/72), averaged with the official annual first-visit weights among reporting employees age 15+. The estimator is anchored: it reproduces the published all-ages earnings tables of the 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24 reports across all 27 cells (residence x sex) within 0.03%, with exactly matching sample counts. Rupees are nominal, so levels mostly reflect inflation; the comparable story is the relative gap - Muslim salaried workers earn 16-23% below the all-India average in every round (2023-24: INR 17,747 vs INR 21,067), and the gap has not closed over the seven rounds. Muslim sample: 4,070-5,122 earnings reporters per round. Religion is self-reported (NSO unit-data rider: indicative, no sub-state estimates). Year=Y is the start of the Jul-Y to Jun-(Y+1) reference period.
