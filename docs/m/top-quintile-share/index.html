About this measurement

Definition. Of all people in a community, the share living in households whose spending (MPCE) places them in the top 20% of Indians. If prosperity were spread evenly, every community would sit at 20%. This reads the whole spending distribution, not the average: Muslims are NOT over-represented among India's poorest fifth (20.5%, at par), but only 13.7% reach the top fifth - the distribution is squeezed below the top.

Methodology. Computed from HCES 2023-24 unit-level microdata (NSO, 2.61 lakh households) via the MoSPI NADA API, with the same validated MPCE machinery as the spending card: person-weighted national MPCE quintile cuts (Rs 2,903 / 3,677 / 4,632 / 6,302 per person per month), then each community's population share within each fifth. Every all-India quintile share equals 20.0 by construction, which is the built-in validation. The full distribution (Muslim, % in Q1 poorest to Q5 richest): 20.5, 24.0, 22.7, 19.1, 13.7. The urban gap is the sharpest cut: 26.3% of urban Muslims reach the national top fifth vs 45.3% of urban Hindus. The "Urban vs rural" tab uses the SAME national cuts, so it reads as the share of the community's urban (rural) population reaching the national top fifth. NSO unit-data rider: religion is self-reported and unverified and the survey is designed for MPCE estimation, so the religion split is indicative and no sub-state estimates are made.

Sources. HCES 2023-24 (NSO unit-level data). Data file: top-quintile-share.csv.
