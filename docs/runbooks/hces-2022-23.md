## Critical methodological gap: no religion crosstabs in published tables

**HCES 2022-23 does not publish MPCE by religion in either the factsheet or the full Report 591.** Religion of the head of household is collected as survey variable Q4.11 (Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Jainism, Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Others, Not reported), but the published tables aggregate consumption by sector (rural/urban), state, social group (SC/ST/OBC/Other), and wealth fractile — not by religion.

For Muslim MPCE specifically, you need:
1. **Unit-level recode files** from https://microdata.gov.in/nada43/index.php/catalog/224 (free registration). Each household record has religion code + consumption expenditure. Compute religion-by-MPCE crosstabs in code.
2. Or wait for a separate NSO subject report (none planned).
3. Or use a research paper that has computed these (e.g., PRICE consumer survey, CMIE — but these use different sampling).

**Status of `mpce` canonical metric:** stub. L1 sources are archived; the unit-level recode step is the next unblock.

## What CAN be extracted from the published tables

- National + state MPCE (all-religion combined) — from factsheet
- State-wise MPCE breakdowns
- MPCE by social group (SC/ST/OBC/Other)
- Food share of expenditure
- Expenditure by quintile

These are dashboard-usable as background context but not Muslim-disaggregated.

## Known issues

- The 2011-12 round and 2022-23 round used different questionnaires; not directly comparable. NSS officially treats them as a methodology break.
- Unit-level processing is the only path to religion crosstabs; no published table fills this gap.
- HCES 2023-24 may follow the same pattern (no published religion crosstabs).
